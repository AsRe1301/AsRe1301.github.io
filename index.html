AsRe Uploader

A private, non-commercial tool that uploads videos to my own YouTube channel.

What the app does

AsRe Uploader is a small script that runs locally on my own computer. It automates one task: uploading finished video files, together with their title, description, tags and privacy setting, to my own YouTube channel using the official YouTube Data API v3.

The tool is not offered to the public. There are no user accounts, no sign-up and no other users – the only Google account ever connected is my own.

Google permissions

The app requests exactly one OAuth scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload – “Manage your YouTube videos” (upload only)

It does not read, analyse or manage any other data of the YouTube or Google account (no comments, analytics, subscribers, playlists, e-mail or contacts).

How data is handled

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only locally on my computer.
  • There is no server, no database and no third-party service involved – data flows only between my computer and Google/YouTube.
  • No data is sold, shared or used for advertising or for training AI models.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
